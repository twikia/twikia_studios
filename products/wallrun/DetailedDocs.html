1. Architecture & Feature Breakdown

Architecture & Core Systems

WallRun provides UWallRunComponent for wall detection, along-wall velocity projection, corner-angle transitions, vertical velocity dampening, cooldown management, and directional wall jumps, paired with UWallRunCameraFXComponent for smooth camera roll tilt and FOV widening.

Entry supports two modes: automatic airborne wall detection (bAutoDetectWalls = true) or hold-to-wall-run via IA_WallRun. Wall jumping can be triggered automatically via IA_WallJump or manually through TryWallJump(). When assigning IA_WallRun or IA_WallJump on the component, InputMappingContext is optional if your character or PlayerController already registers an Enhanced Input Mapping Context containing those actions.

Wall Jump Launch Math & Contracts

ComputeWallJumpVelocity blends WallPushOffForce along the wall normal, WallUpForce vertically, and ForwardMaintainFactor along the player's directional input, scaled proportionally to the character's JumpZVelocity relative to JumpHeight. Optional inward/outward push scaling and inward velocity stripping prevent awkward wall-clipping or weak hops.

Implement IWallRunCharacterContract on custom characters to supply movement input and jump eligibility, or IWallRunListener to react to wall-run enter, exit, and launch events.

1. Architecture & Feature Breakdown

Modular & All-In-One Architecture

MovementSuite is built on a shared UMovementBaseComponent foundation with independent state components (Sprint, Slide, WallRun, Grapple, Dive, SlowFall, FastFlying) and ability components (Dashes, Jump, Jetpack). You can attach individual components for a lightweight setup or drop in UMovementSuiteComponent to orchestrate the full traversal suite together.

UMovementComboManagerComponent tracks chained traversal moves with decay timers and multiplier hooks, while UMovementTimeSlowManagerComponent and UMovementTimeDilationSubsystem coordinate global or local bullet-time transitions cleanly without fighting external time-dilation writers.

Camera FX, Input & Contracts

UMovementSuiteCameraFXComponent and UMovementCameraFXComponent drive per-state FOV shifts, camera roll, and offset transitions across wall running, sliding, sprinting, grappling, diving, and dashing.

Every component supports both direct Enhanced Input asset assignment in Details and manual Blueprint/C++ triggers, plus IMovementCharacterContract and IMovementListener interfaces for custom rigs.

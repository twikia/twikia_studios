1. Architecture & Feature Breakdown

Architecture & Core Systems

Grapple is split into three focused runtime building blocks: UGrappleComponent (state machine, aim raycasts, exponential pull and WASD perpendicular swing forces, auto-release distance, duration timer, and cooldown timer), UGrappleCameraFXComponent (smooth FOV widening and lateral camera roll toward the tether), and AGrappleSplineActor (procedural sagging rope spline and hook mesh).

Every tunable is exposed on the Details panel and editable at runtime from Blueprint or C++. Enhanced Input bindings are optional: assign InputMappingContext and IA_Grapple on UGrappleComponent for zero-graph input handling, or leave IA_Grapple unset and drive TryEnterGrapple() and ExitGrapple() directly from custom Blueprint or C++ input handlers.

Physics, Framing & Contracts

All pull and swing impulses pass through VectorFrameRateCorrection(BaseImpulse, DeltaTime), normalizing movement to a 60 FPS reference step so swing arcs and pull distances remain consistent across 30, 60, 120, and 240 FPS.

Custom pawns or rope actors can implement IGrappleCharacterContract, IGrappleSplineContract, and IGrappleListener to override view origin, aim direction, anchor socket location, or react to attach/detach events without subclassing.

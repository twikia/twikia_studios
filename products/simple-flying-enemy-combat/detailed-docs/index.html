1. Architecture & Feature Breakdown

Combat & Projectile Systems

Player and enemy ranged combat shares UBulletSpawnerComponent and ABullet subclasses (ABullet, ABulletBomb, ABulletSaw) driven by UDataAsset gun presets (standard rifle, multi-split shotgun, explosive launcher, ricochet and pierce saw). UGunComponent, UGunCosmeticComponent, and UShootInputComponent separate firing logic, muzzle/recoil cosmetics, and Enhanced Input binding.

UHealthComponent manages HP, armor absorption, dodge chance, passive regeneration, critical hitboxes (UCritBoxComponent), and crimson screen-space feedback (UDamageHUDWidget), while UHitFlashComponent and UDamagePopupComponent provide emissive hit-flashes and floating damage/CRIT numbers.

Flying Enemy AI Archetypes & Steering

Six distinct archetypes (Persistor, BombBot, MachineGunner, NailGunner, Stalker, LaserBallBot) run on UFlyingEnemyStateMachine and specialized logic components inheriting UFlyingEnemyBaseLogic / UFlyingAirBaseLogic.

UFlyingEnemyFunctionLibrary provides obstacle-avoidance ray steering, constricting spiral orbits, and predictive lead-aiming helpers reusable in both C++ and Blueprints.

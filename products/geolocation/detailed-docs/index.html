1. Architecture & Feature Breakdown

Subsystem, Async Requests & Listener

UGeoLocationSubsystem is a UGameInstanceSubsystem implementing IGeoLocationAccess for unified permission requests, continuous GPS/network location tracking, circular geofence registration, and Play Integrity checks across Android, iOS (experimental), and Win64 editor stubs.

Use UGeoLocationAsync::RequestLocationOperation in Blueprints for latent request-completion flows, or attach UGeoLocationListener to any actor to receive OnLocation, OnGeofence, and OnError broadcasts automatically.

Spoof-Risk Scoring & Math

GeoMath::Assess evaluates each incoming FGeoFix against FGeoRiskConfig thresholds (MaximumSpeed, MaximumAccuracy, MockWeight, RootWeight, SpeedWeight) and outputs an FGeoRisk score plus FGameplayTagContainer reasons.

UGeoLocationLibrary::SimulateFix allows development-only synthetic fix injection directly in PIE without a physical mobile device.
